THE BUSINESS CONTEXT

Technology decisions need a business frame.

New tools can create possibilities, but they also introduce costs, dependencies, and change. Before choosing a platform or starting an initiative, it helps to understand what success should look like and what needs to happen first.

Technology strategy consulting brings those questions together. We consider the current environment, business objectives, and practical constraints to help establish a direction you can explain and act on.
