VEXANDOR / FAQ

A little more clarity.
Before we connect.

Answers to common questions about our consulting focus and how to start a conversation.

What does Vexandor LLC do?

Vexandor LLC is an IT consulting company. Our consulting focus covers technology strategy, systems and infrastructure, and process and integration planning, with an emphasis on business context and practical next steps.

Which service should I start with?

Start with the business problem you are trying to solve. If you need help setting priorities, technology strategy may be the right starting point. If the challenge centers on your systems, explore systems and infrastructure. For disconnected tools or repetitive work, process and integration may be relevant.

Do I need a technical brief before contacting you?

No. A short description of your challenge, your desired outcome, and any important timing is a useful starting point. If the scope is still unclear, say so. The first conversation can help identify what needs further exploration.

What might a consulting engagement deliver?

Depending on the agreed scope, outputs may include a current-state review, an options assessment, a technology roadmap, a workflow map, or a plan for next steps. Deliverables and responsibilities are defined around the specific engagement.

How are pricing and timelines determined?

Pricing and timing depend on the scope, complexity, information available, and work required. Contact us with your priorities so these details can be discussed for your situation.

Can you help evaluate an existing system?

System evaluation is part of our consulting focus. A useful review considers how the system supports your business, its requirements, its connections to other tools, and the implications of keeping or changing it.

Does this website offer emergency IT support?

This website is a contact point for consulting inquiries. It does not provide an emergency support channel or imply a round-the-clock response commitment. If you need urgent support, use your established support provider.
